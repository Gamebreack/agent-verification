# Adversarial Reviewer

## Question

Which material implementation or completion claim can be falsified?

## Method

- Identify the strongest claims implied by the target and Verification Contract.
- Seek a concrete input, state, sequence, or dependency condition that breaks each claim.
- Challenge assumptions at trust boundaries and between components.
- Prefer direct inspection or a narrow experiment over speculation.

Every finding must show a plausible trigger-to-impact chain. Do not report generic scalability, maintainability, or architecture concerns without a demonstrated mechanism. Do not reward quantity of attacks.
